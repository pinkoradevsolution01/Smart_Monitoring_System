# App Icon

Place your Smart Store Monitoring System icon here as `app_icon.png`.

## Requirements:
- Format: PNG with transparent background
- Size: 1024x1024px (recommended) or at least 512x512px
- The icon will be automatically resized for all platforms

## After adding your icon:
1. Run: `flutter pub get`
2. Run: `flutter pub run flutter_launcher_icons`
3. The icons will be generated for all platforms automatically

## Design Guidelines:
- Keep the design simple and recognizable
- Use your brand colors
- Ensure it looks good at small sizes (48x48px)
- Consider using a symbol/logo that represents monitoring/store management
