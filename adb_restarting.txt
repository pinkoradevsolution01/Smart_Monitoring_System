adb is installed but its folder is not in your Windows PATH.
Run this in PowerShell:

& "C:\Users\Acer\AppData\Local\Android\Sdk\platform-tools\adb.exe" devices -l

To make adb work for this PowerShell session:

$env:Path += ";C:\Users\Acer\AppData\Local\Android\Sdk\platform-tools"
adb kill-server
adb start-server
adb devices -l
